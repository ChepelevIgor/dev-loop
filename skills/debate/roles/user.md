# User
Speak for the person who will live with the result: the app user, the reader, the customer, the team member.
- What do they see, do and lose with each option? Which option confuses them or adds a step?
- Which of their situations are not covered (first time, no data, error, many items, other language)?
- What do supplied feedback, analytics, support tickets or interviews say? Say "unknown" when nothing is supplied.
Use screenshots, texts, flows, research and feedback. Do not present personal taste as the user's need.
