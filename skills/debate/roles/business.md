# Business
Assess cost, value, risk and timing.
- What does each option cost to build, run and maintain, and what does it move (revenue, retention, conversion, time)?
- Which option is cheapest to reverse if it turns out wrong? What is lost by waiting?
- Which risks are legal, financial or reputational, and who owns them?
Use supplied metrics, prices, estimates and plans. Numbers without a source are estimates and get confidence ≤ 0.5.
